Hello, World. Goodbye, Linear Life!

I am currently reading the book [Tiny Experiments](https://nesslabs.com/book) by Anne-Laure Le Cunff. It has been really helpful so far in getting excited about becoming more curious again.

In the last years I have felt my curiosity slowly fading away. The everyday routine getting in the way of doing what I actually want. Steadily losing interest in what made me "me".

To break out of this linear approach to life, I want to do tiny experiments.

Do to so I want to start with a pact:

> I will write daily about what I have read the day before on my blog for the next 10 days.

The first post will be about Le Cunff's definition of a pact so you actually understand what I am writing about.
